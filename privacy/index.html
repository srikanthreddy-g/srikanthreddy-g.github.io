Park & Pick Up Privacy Policy

Who we are

Park & Pick Up is published by G Srikanth Reddy. For questions about this policy or requests about your data, email ebowbowproductions@gmail.com.

Playing the game

You can play without creating a Park & Pick Up account. The game saves your progress, coins, boosters, and settings on your device. A diagnostic file may also be stored on your device to help investigate crashes. If you send us a diagnostic file or contact us, we receive the information you choose to share.

In-app purchases

When purchases are available, Google Play (on Android) or the Apple App Store (on iOS) handles checkout and payment details.

  • On Android, to verify or restore a Google Play purchase, the game sends the purchase token, product, quantity, and a randomly generated installation identifier to our verifier on Google Cloud. The verifier checks the purchase with Google Play and stores a hash of the token, the installation identifier, the product, quantity, and the verification time to prevent duplicate rewards and help resolve purchase issues; it does not store the raw purchase token in the receipt database. The verifier and hosting providers may process standard connection information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect their services.
  • On iOS, purchases and purchase restoration are processed directly through Apple StoreKit on your device, and transaction records are verified on-device using Apple’s signed transaction information.

Ads and privacy choices

Park & Pick Up is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Some versions offer advertising through Unity LevelPlay and participating ad providers, including Google. Before requesting ads, the game checks Google’s advertising privacy settings (and on iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission where applicable). Ad and consent providers may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and privacy choices to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. You can manage available advertising privacy choices in Settings and in your device’s privacy settings.

Retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it, subject to any device backup settings. We retain purchase verification records as needed to verify purchases, prevent duplicate fulfillment, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We retain support correspondence as needed to handle your request and related disputes or legal obligations. To ask for access, correction, or deletion of data we hold, email us. We may ask for enough information to verify the request and locate the record. We may keep records needed for legal obligations or to protect against duplicate claims. Clearing game data or deleting a verification record does not delete purchase records held by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Children’s privacy and parents

Park & Pick Up is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 shared personal information with us or made a purchase in error, email us so we can investigate. For payments and refunds processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, you can also use Google Play or Apple Support.
