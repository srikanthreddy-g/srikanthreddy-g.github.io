Quick FAQ

  • How do I restore Remove Ads on a new device? Open Settings → Restore purchases (or the in-game Shop) while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google Play account.
  • How do boosters work? Use + Stop to open an extra pickup bay, Send away to return an empty waiting vehicle to its bay, and Swap to cycle waiting passengers.
  • How do I manage ad privacy choices? Open in-game Settings or adjust your device’s Tracking / Privacy settings.
